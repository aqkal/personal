# Campus modeling review, 6 September 2026

The original low-poly campus is generated by `scripts/blender/environment.py` and `scripts/blender/studios.py`. The assembled editable master is `campus.blend`. The fifteen named building, landscape and detail `.blend` files contain isolated asset geometry and their own review camera, sun and ground. An optional `campus-source-review.json` QA report recorded fresh-open isolation checks during this dated review; it is intentionally excluded and is not a build input.

The geometry pass corrects game-to-Blender yaw and applies dimensions before metre-scale bevels. Great Court has continuous covered arches, curved masonry spandrels, an open Forgan Smith gate, stepped tower, four-sided slits and window surrounds on all wing elevations. Qualia retains its construction identity, DSR its design atelier display panels and sawtooth roof, and BucklerX its split laboratory volumes. Cafe pergola beams are supported, the terrace has actual chairs and tables, and the bridge stays sit outside the walking deck.

The final campus GLB contains 63,412 triangles in 10 material batches, with no textures or external buffers. It is 2,076,196 bytes before HTTP compression and approximately 175 KB with gzip. Small framing elements use square edges; larger silhouettes retain bevels. Authored colors are direct glTF material factors multiplied by grayscale vertex AO.

## Collision contract

`public/assets/game/campus.metadata.json` contains `authoredLayout.colliderSchema.version = 2` and 373 geometry-derived primary-solid descriptors, including the 18 actual tree trunks and 200 conservative stepped arch overhead descriptors. Each collider has `id`, `type`, `center: [x,z]`, `baseY`, `height`, and `rotation` in Three.js positive-Y radians. Boxes add local `half: [halfX,halfZ]`; cylinders add `radius`. Building faces remain closed exhibit facades, apart from the real Forgan gateway and open supported spaces. Sloping bridge deck collision comes from the shared bridge profile in the runtime. The road-side bench was moved from (-24,7) to (-24,10.5).

All preview images were rendered and visually inspected in Blender. They are generated local outputs under `assets-source/` and can be recreated with `npm run assets:build`; they are intentionally excluded from repository inputs. Independent Sol review found the continuous cloister bays readable and the cafe structure coherent; its DSR roof-light proportion note was addressed. These source reviews do not constitute browser gameplay or physics validation.
